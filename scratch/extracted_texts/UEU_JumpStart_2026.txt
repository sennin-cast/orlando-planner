Welcome to Universal Epic Universe
Universal Epic Universe is a brand-new theme park in Orlando, FL. Just a hop, skip and a jump 
away from Universal Orlando Resort’s three additional theme parks — Universal Studios Florida, 
Universal Islands of Adventure and Universal Volcano Bay — Universal Epic Universe transports 
guests into brand-new stories, including the “Fantastic Beasts” and “How to Train Your Dragon” 
films, the famed and fearsome Universal Monsters and the perfectly pixelated SUPER NINTENDO 
WORLD™.
Enter the iconic Chronos at Epic Universe and you’ll find five separate worlds, each uniquely 
themed with its own attractions, activities, characters and food. Throughout Epic Universe, you’ll 
find over 30 restaurants, 11 rides, 2 shows, 12 spell locations, 20 characters, 20 stores and so much 
more! At Universal Epic Universe, you truly become a part of the stories being told within Celestial 
Park, Dark Universe, SUPER NINTENDO WORLD™, How to Train Y our Dragon - Isle of Berk and The 
Wizarding World of Harry Potter™ - Ministry of Magic™.
Plus, Universal Orlando Resort opened three brand-new hotels within close proximity to Epic 
Universe. In fact, you’ll find Universal’s very first theme park entrance within a hotel! 
Universal Epic Universe is a short bus ride away from Universal Orlando Resort’s other theme 
parks and hotels, too. 
Within this guide, you’ll find all of the information you need to plan your epic vacation, from what 
lies within each world, to where and how long to stay, to our top recommendations on what you 
won’t want to miss.

Transportation
Complimentary buses are available from your 
Universal hotel and Universal CityWalk. If you’re 
driving, Epic Universe has its own surface parking 
lot, which offers standard, prime, valet and 
accessible parking, as well as electric vehicle 
charging (additional fees apply).
Guest Services
Guest Services can be found immediately to 
the right side after entering the park. There are 
four Concierge Huts located near the portals of 
each world. Each Concierge Hut can also help 
with Fuel Rods, large lockers, digital information 
boards, Universal Express Passes, dining 
reservations and Coca-Cola Freestyle cups.
Lockers
All-day lockers are available to rent on the left 
side near the park entrance or at the Concierge 
Huts. Complimentary ride lockers are available 
within the queues of Hiccup’s Wing Gliders, 
Monsters Unchained: The Frankenstein 
Experiment and Stardust Racers. Stardust Racers 
does not allow any loose items and requires 
metal detection. 
Wheelchairs and Strollers
Wheelchair, ECV and stroller rentals can be 
found immediately to the left side after entering 
the park. The following daily rates are manual 
wheelchairs ($15), regular ECVs ($75), canopy 
ECVs ($95) and strollers ($40). 
*Rates subject to change
Accessibility
Universal Orlando Resort provides 
accommodations via its Attractions Assistance 
Pass, which is obtained by registering for the 
IBCCES Individual Accessibility Card (IAC) at 
AccessibilityCard.org between 30 days and 48 
hours before visiting. Guests who only need to 
use their mobility device do not need to apply 
for the IAC. While stairs are prevalent at the 
park, all queues and worlds are accessible.
Family Care Center & First Aid
There are two First Aid locations — one near 
the front of the park by Guest Relations and 
the other in the back next to the Family Care 
Center. The Family Care Center has amenities 
including quiet rooms, a kitchenette, companion 
restrooms and adult changing tables. 
Child Swap
If you have tadpoles who don’t meet a height 
requirement or are unwilling to go on a ride, 
you can utilize Child Swap. Enter a ride queue 
as a family. Once you get closer to the loading 
station, tell a team member you’d like to wait in 
the Family Room, where you can stay with the 
tadpoles while the rest of your party rides. Once 
they’re done, adults can switch places so you 
don’t have to miss out or wait again in line.
Photo Validation
Photo Validation is now being used to verify 
tickets and passes at park entrances and 
Universal Express Passes in select queues. It 
is completely optional — you can opt out, but 
prepare to present your valid ID and tickets. 
The new locker system also uses a similar facial 
recognition feature. Now you don’t have to keep 
your park ticket on you, unless you prefer using 
it to lock and unlock your locker.
