TIPS
At least one water park is open year-round 
(while the other is closed for maintenance). 
Both water parks operate during the  
summer season. 
Walt Disney World® Resort hotel guests get 
FREE water park admission on arrival day 
from May 26 to Sept. 8, 2026, and in  
summer 2027!
Complimentary life jackets and towels are 
available for all water park guests.
Parking is free at the water parks. Disney  
bus transportation is available, but you 
will need to transfer at Disney Springs® to 
Disney’s Typhoon Lagoon Water Park, or may 
need to transfer at Disney’s Animal Kingdom® 
Theme Park to Disney’s Blizzard Beach  
Water Park (depending on your resort). 
Disney’s Typhoon Lagoon Water Park
• Sand Pail Sundae at Happy Landings
• Mini Donuts by Joffrey’s Coffee
• Elote Nachos at Leaning Palms 
Disney’s Blizzard Beach Water Park
• Sand Pail Sundae at I.C. Expeditions
• Mini Donuts by Joffrey’s Coffee
• Rice Bowls at Lottawatta Lodge
Mobile Order Available
Arrive early, especially if you’re driving (the 
parking lots can fill up!) or need multiple 
beach chairs in prime locations.  
Avoid bringing valuables, but standard 
($10) and large ($15) lockers are available to 
rent for the day. 
Mobile ordering is available at Leaning 
Palms and Typhoon Tilly’s at Disney’s 
Typhoon Lagoon Water Park and Avalunch, 
Lottawatta Lodge and Warming Hut at 
Disney’s Blizzard Beach Water Park. 
Y ou can bring your own food into the water 
parks (loose ice, dry ice, alcohol and glass 
are not permitted).  
Wear water shoes to walk around the water 
parks. Y ou can wear them on most rides, 
unlike flip-flops. 
Don’t froget to bring your own sunscreen! 
Sundries are available if you need to 
purchase in the park. 
Hold onto young tadpoles in the wave 
pools. The Typhoon Lagoon Surf Pool is 
particularly powerful!

on select nights after the parks close to the general public, 
including access to a third virtual queue window, if in use

The Advance Dining Reservation System, ADR for short, is a fancy name for making 
dining reservations at Disney’s table-service restaurants. There are hundreds of dining 
opportunities on property, ranging from dinner shows to character dining. If you’re not 
booking that far in advance, don’t stress — there are some good quick-service options 
and restaurants that often have last-minute availability!

All guests (even those staying off-site) 
can make Walt Disney World® Resort 
dining reservations!
Advance Dining Reservations can be 
made in the My Disney Experience app 
60 days in advance of your trip.
Most reservations become available 
around 5:45 a.m. EST or by calling 
407-WDW-DINE at 7 a.m. EST.
Walt Disney World® Resort hotel guests 
can book ADRs 60 days in advance for 
the length of their stay (up to 10 days).
Y ou can make a reservation online for 
parties up to 20; your whole party does 
not need to be connected in My Disney 
Experience to make a reservation.
Select restaurants require a deposit 
upfront to reserve your spot.
Valid park admission (and a theme park 
reservation, if applicable) is required for 
in-park dining locations.
Y ou can cancel a reservation up to 2 
hours before your reservation time 
without penalty at most restaurants. 
Cancellations within the 2 hour window 
may result in a no-show charge, per 
person.
Make sure you have the latest version 
of the My Disney Experience app.
Have your credit card information saved 
in the app. This saves you an extra step 
during the process. 
Y ou can search for reservations within 
a 10-day range. Then you can filter by 
time and location
Work backward for the best availability 
if you are staying on-site.
If at first you don’t succeed, keep
checking up until (and during) your trip.
Don’t froget about the walk-up list, 
available same-day when you’re near 
select restaurants!

DISNEY LIGHTNING LANE SERVICE
A new paid skip-the-line service is available at Walt Disney World® 
Resort — and you can purchase and start reserving your rides before 
your trip! Enter Lightning Lane Passes.
Exact pricing depends on the 
Theme Park and date (Lightning 
Lane Multi Pass and Lightning 
Lane Premier Pass) or attraction 
and date (Lightning Lane 
Single Pass). 
To view pricing, proceed to any 
Purchase button found on the 
home page, Tip Board or the 
Lightning Lane Passes section of 
the main menu in the My Disney 
Experience app.
Once you select a date, you’ll 
see the pricing for both Multi 
Pass and Single Pass by Theme 
Park.
Y ou can purchase Lightning Lane 
Multi Pass and Single Pass in the 
same transaction for each day.
Lightning Lane Premier Pass 
pricing and transactions are 
separate.
For advance Lightning Lane 
Multi Pass selections, attractions 
and experiences are split into 
two tiers at Magic Kingdom® 
Park, EPCOT® and Disney’s 
Hollywood Studios®. 
Be sure to prioritize your advance 
selections accordingly, as you 
can choose 1 from Tier 1 and 
choose 2 (or all 3) from Tier 2.
Multi Pass and Single Pass arrival 
windows are automatically 
selected, but you can modify any 
time window*. Once you make 
your selections, you have five 
minutes to confirm the times.
Once you’re in the park and 
redeem your first Lightning 
Lane Multi Pass selection, you 
can make another from either 
Tier 1 or 2* in the same park — 
or a different park if you have 
park hopping capabilities. Just 
remember, “use one, get one”!
*subject to availability

TIPS AND TRICKS
Here are our top tips and tricks for making the very 
most of your time at Walt Disney World® Resort!
Decide in advance where you want 
to go. Use our list of attractions so you can 
decide what your priorities are for each park. 
Make planning your trip a family affair, and
let everyone pick a couple of rides that are 
must-dos. 
Pick the best day to visit. Use our 
Crowd Calendar to identify the best dates to 
visit the Walt Disney World® Resort and then 
the best parks to visit on each day of your 
trip. Since Magic Kingdom® Park is always 
the busiest park, try to visit on a green day.
Download our Orlando Theme Park 
Day app. It offers key showtimes, crowd 
levels, wait times and the optimal time to visit 
each attraction.
Utilize on-site perks. If you’re staying at
a Walt Disney World® Resort hotel, you can 
enter any of the theme parks 30 minutes 
early on each day of your stay (valid 
admission required). Likewise, hotel guests 
are able to purchase Lightning Lane Passes 
up to seven days in advance for their entire 
length of stay. All other guests can purchase 
up to three days in advance. 
Arrive before the parks open. The first 
few hours are the least busy times in the 
park, and you can experience a lot more 
when crowds are low.
Consider Disney Lightning Lane 
Passes. These paid services enable guests 
to wait in an expedited line for popular 
attractions. The price varies by date and 
must be purchased through the My Disney 
Experience app for each member of your 
party. Remember, this service is toadally 
optional and all attractions still have a free 
means of entry — either by standby or virtual 
queue (if in use).
Experience the most popular rides 
with the lowest durations first. 
Y ou can follow one of our park plans or 
customize your own. If you aren’t a family 
of early risers or you want more flexibility 
than a park plan offers, use our general 
map to decide which rides to visit at any 
given time.
Take a break in the middle of the 
day. Especially if you’ve got young 
tadpoles, we suggest taking a break in 
the afternoon and then coming back in 
the evening for dinner and fireworks.
