SeaWorld® Orlando 1-Day Park Plan
PARK PLAN
We frogs feel right at home in this lush, tropical paradise of a theme park that features animal attractions, exhibits, aquariums 
and shows. While there are some world-class rides at SeaWorld® Orlando, the animal shows are what most people come to 
see. Show times are staggered throughout the day, typically starting in the late morning and ending in the late afternoon. 
During summer and Christmas Celebration, the park is open later and features additional shows and a fireworks finale. We 
recommend arriving at shows at least 20 minutes prior to show time for the best seats. 
Our favorite exhibit is Pacific Point Preserve, where California sea lions and harbor seals swim and play among the waves 
and bask in the sun. You can even get a chance to feed these flippered friends! Nearby is Journey to Atlantis®, a combination 
dark ride and water ride with wait times that spike during the hottest months. Manta® and Mako™ are not to be missed if you 
are a coaster fan — they are two of the best in Florida! The fear factor keeps many away, but you’ll still find the lowest waits 
within the first and last two hours of park operation. 
Planning your day largely depends on the number of available shows and show times. For this reason, we’ve included the 
following list of park priorities.

We’ve created this one-day park plan for SeaWorld® Orlando so you can 
experience the most popular rides before the day’s shows begin, and allow 
yourself time to experience the shows, aquariums and exhibits. This plan 
assumes you do not have Quick Queue Unlimited, and are starting at park 
opening and finishing in the evening. Because the number of SeaWorld® 
Orlando shows and show times vary depending on the season, you may 
need to adjust the plan to fit current show times. We recommend checking 
the SeaWorld® Orlando website a few weeks before your visit to see the 
expected show schedule.
SeaWorld® Orlando 1-Day Park Plan
1. Ride Manta®
2. Ride Journey to Atlantis®
3. Ride Kraken®
4. Visit SeaWorld Rescue Center 
5. Visit Stingray Lagoon®
6. Watch Dolphin Adventures at Dolphin Theater
7. Have lunch at Voyager’s Smokehouse
8. Visit Pacific Point Preserve® 
9. Watch Sea Lion and Otter Spotlight at Sea Lion and  
Otter Stadium
10. Ride Penguin Trek
11. Visit Shark Encounter®
12. Ride Mako®
13. Ride Infinity Falls™
14. Visit Sesame Street Land™ (if you have children  
under age 6)
15. Visit Wild Arctic® Habitat
16. Visit Orca Underwater Viewing
17. Have dinner at Altitude Burgers
18. Watch Orca Encounter at Orca Stadium
19. Ride Ice Breaker™
20. Ride Pipeline: The Surf Coaster
21. Visit Dolphin Nursery
