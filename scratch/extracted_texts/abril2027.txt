Our Crowd Calendar is built using years of historical wait-time trends, seasonal travel patterns,
holidays, special events, operating hours and other factors that influence crowd levels. While no
crowd prediction tool is perfect, our team continuously analyzes available data and updates
predictions as conditions change to help travelers make informed, up-to-date decisions.
What do the Crowd Calendar levels mean?
Crowd levels range from 1 to 10, with lower numbers indicating lighter crowds and shorter average
wait times.
When is the best time to visit the Orlando theme parks?
The best time to visit Orlando depends on your priorities, whether you're looking for lower crowds,
seasonal events, milder weather or longer park hours. You can use our Crowd Calendar to compare
all of these factors in one spot to find the best dates for you. For a more thorough breakdown, check
out our guides to the Best Times to Visit Walt Disney World  Resort and Best Times to Visit Universal
Orlando.
